### About this site

Hantavirus Updates explains hantavirus in plain language: what it is, how people catch it, how to stay safe and what happened in the 2026 cruise ship outbreak. It started in May 2026 during the outbreak and is now kept as a reference.

We are not a health authority. Everything here is drawn from public health agencies and linked to the original source. Pages show the date they were last reviewed.

### Disclaimer

This site is for general information only and is not medical advice. If you are worried about your health, contact a doctor. If you have trouble breathing, call your local emergency number.
